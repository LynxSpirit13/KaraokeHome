# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want KaraokeHome to be a welcoming, respectful project for contributors of all experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, ask questions, and explain disagreements calmly.
- Give actionable feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy and copyright.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
